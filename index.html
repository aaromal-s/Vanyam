UNDERSTANDING KERALA'S FORESTS

A Land Shaped by Forests

Kerala lies along the southwestern coast of India, cradled by the Western Ghats — one of the world's eight hottest biodiversity hotspots. The state's forests, fed by two annual monsoon seasons, support an extraordinary variety of life.

From tropical evergreen rainforests at lower elevations to stunted shola forests in the high mountains, Kerala's woodlands are not just home to thousands of species — they are the source of almost every river in the state, sustaining agriculture, drinking water and coastal ecosystems.

FOREST ECOSYSTEMS

Many Forests, One Landscape

Kerala's diverse topography and climate create a mosaic of distinct forest ecosystems, each with unique characteristics and wildlife.

Tropical Evergreen Forest

Dense, multi-layered forests receiving over 2,500 mm of annual rainfall. Towering trees reach 40–60 metres, creating a thick canopy that blocks most sunlight.

High rainfall Dense canopy Rich biodiversity

Moist Deciduous Forest

Forests where many trees shed their leaves during the dry season. Found in areas with 1,500–2,500 mm of rainfall, these forests host teak, rosewood and bamboo.

Seasonal Teak & Rosewood Moderate rainfall

Shola Forest

Unique stunted evergreen forests found above 1,500 metres in the Western Ghats. Shola forests grow in sheltered valleys between grassy hilltops and are critically important for water catchment.

High altitude Endemic species Water catchment

Montane Grasslands

High-altitude grasslands found above 2,000 metres, interspersed with shola forest patches. These grasslands are home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and many endemic plants.

Above 2000m Nilgiri Tahr Cool climate

Bamboo & Riparian Ecosystems

Bamboo thickets and riparian forests along rivers and streams form critical wildlife corridors and prevent soil erosion. They support unique communities of fish, amphibians and birds.

River corridors Erosion control Amphibians

INTERACTIVE STORY

A Journey Through Kerala's Forests

rainy

Monsoon Arrives

Dark clouds roll across the Arabian Sea each June, heralding the southwest monsoon. Within days, Kerala's western slopes are drenched in torrential rain that will shape the forests for the coming year.

park

Forest Canopy

Rain cascades through layers of the forest canopy — emergent trees, the main canopy, understory and forest floor. Each layer is a microhabitat supporting different species of plants, insects, birds and mammals.

pets

Wildlife Awakens

The monsoon triggers breeding seasons, migrations and feeding frenzies. Elephants move to higher ground, frogs sing in explosive choruses, and the forest floor erupts with fungi, insects and new plant growth.

water_drop

Streams & Waterfalls

Thousands of ephemeral streams appear, feeding waterfalls that plunge down the Western Ghats. These streams carry nutrients downhill and form the headwaters of Kerala's 44 rivers.

landscape

Western Ghats Summit

Above 2,000 metres, shola forests and montane grasslands are wrapped in cloud and mist. These high-altitude ecosystems are home to endemic species found nowhere else on Earth, like the Nilgiri Tahr.

shield

Conservation

Protecting Kerala's forests means protecting water, wildlife, climate regulation and the livelihoods of millions. National parks, tiger reserves and community forests are all part of this effort.

eco

Did You Know?

Silent Valley National Park in Kerala is one of the last remaining patches of virgin tropical evergreen forest in India. It was saved from a hydroelectric project in the 1980s thanks to one of India's earliest environmental campaigns.

CHALLENGES

What Threatens Kerala's Forests?

Understanding the threats is the first step towards protecting these irreplaceable ecosystems.

local_fire_department

Forest Fires

Summer fires, often caused by human activity, destroy vegetation, kill wildlife and degrade soil quality in forest areas.

park

Deforestation

Conversion of forest land for agriculture, plantations and development continues to reduce Kerala's natural forest cover.

construction

Habitat Fragmentation

Roads, settlements and plantations break up continuous forest into isolated patches, restricting wildlife movement.

thermostat

Climate Change

Shifting monsoon patterns, rising temperatures and extreme weather events threaten forest health and species survival.

grass

Invasive Species

Exotic plants like Senna spectabilis and Mikania micrantha spread aggressively, displacing native vegetation.

pets

Human–Wildlife Conflict

As settlements expand into forest fringes, encounters between humans and wild animals — especially elephants — increase.

delete

Pollution

Plastic waste, chemical runoff from plantations and tourism-related litter degrade forest and river ecosystems.

OUR RESPONSIBILITY

The Forest Needs Our Voice

Conservation isn't just for rangers and scientists. Understanding the crisis and your impact is the first step towards protecting the wild heart of the Western Ghats.

Conservation Triumphs

1970s - 1980s

The Save Silent Valley Movement

One of India's earliest and most successful environmental campaigns. A massive grassroots movement stopped a hydroelectric dam project that would have flooded the pristine Silent Valley, saving the endangered Lion-tailed Macaque and countless endemic species.

Present

Periyar Tiger Reserve Success

Through robust anti-poaching camps, eco-development programs, and local community involvement (turning former poachers into forest protectors), Periyar has become one of the best-managed tiger reserves in the country.

Rules of the Forest (Leave No Trace)

no_meals

Do Not Feed Wildlife

Feeding wild animals alters their natural diet, causes dependency, and increases dangerous human-wildlife conflict.

volume_off

Maintain Silence

Loud noises, music, and shouting cause extreme stress to wildlife and disrupt their communication and hunting patterns.

no_photography

No Flash Photography

Sudden bright flashes disorient nocturnal animals and can cause panic among herds like elephants and deer.

location_off

Avoid Geotagging

Sharing exact locations of endangered species on social media helps poachers track and hunt them down easily.

Your Ecological Impact

shopping_bag

Consumer Choices

Buying certified sustainable timber and avoiding products linked to deforestation helps preserve vital habitats.

co2

eco
